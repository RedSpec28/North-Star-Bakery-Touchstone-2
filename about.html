OUR STORY

Our Neighborhood Bakery

We bake for the neighbors who stop in every morning and the families who return for special occasions.

How It Started

North Star Bakery began with a simple idea: make fresh bread and good pastries for the people who live and work nearby. What began as a small morning baking routine grew into a place for familiar faces, quick breakfasts, and cakes for life's celebrations.

Our Approach to Ingredients

We focus on straightforward recipes, careful preparation, and seasonal choices whenever possible. We aim to work with local suppliers when the ingredients and availability make sense.

If you have a food allergy, please ask about ingredients before ordering. Our shared kitchen may handle wheat, milk, eggs, nuts, and other allergens.

Meet the People Behind the Counter

Our Bakers

The baking crew starts early to prepare dough, check the ovens, and make sure the day's breads and pastries are ready for visitors.

Our Front-Counter Team

The team at the counter helps guests choose a pastry, plan a pickup, and ask questions about ingredients or special occasions.
