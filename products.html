OUR BAKES

Fresh from Our Oven

Our case changes with the season, but you'll always find something made by hand. These are sample price ranges, not online checkout prices.

Breads

Choose an everyday loaf or bring home something for a shared meal.

  • Country bread: Crusty, sliceable loaves ($6-$9).
  • Whole-grain bread: Hearty loaves ($7-$10).
  • Specialty bread: Seasonal flavors ($8-$12).

Pastries

Stop in for a morning pastry or choose a small box for coworkers.

  • Butter pastries: Flaky morning favorites ($3-$5 each).
  • Muffins and sweet rolls: Fresh daily ($3-$6 each).
  • Pastry boxes: A mix to share ($16-$30 per box).

Cakes for Celebrations

Simple birthday cakes and seasonal celebration cakes are available by request. Please include your date, serving count, and preferred flavors when you contact us.

Sample starting range: $30-$75, depending on size and decoration.
